Support

Questions, bugs or a message that missed the mark? Email support@heynudgy.com and we'll get back to you.

Nudgy isn't sending reminders

Open Nudgy and check that Screen Time access and notifications are both allowed (Settings > Screen Time and Settings > Notifications > Nudgy). Then go to Settings in Nudgy and choose Re-configure Tracked Apps.

Which apps can Nudgy track?

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, X and YouTube. The free version tracks up to 3 of them; Premium tracks all 6.

Does Nudgy see what I do in those apps?

No. Nudgy uses Apple's Screen Time framework, which only tells it how long an app has been open, and that data never leaves your phone. See our Privacy Policy.

Managing or cancelling Premium

Nudgy Premium is a monthly subscription billed through your Apple Account. To cancel, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone and choose Nudgy. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Nudgy > Settings > Restore Purchases.
