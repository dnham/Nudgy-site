Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Nudgy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

Information We Collect

Email address: When you sign up for early access on our website, we collect the email address you provide.

Screen time data: Nudgy accesses your device's screen time and app usage data to deliver timely reminders. This data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Notification preferences: Your reminder intervals, tracked-app choices and notification history are stored only on your device.

Message feedback: When you rate a reminder message with a thumbs up or down, Nudgy sends the message text, the name of the app it was about (for example, "Instagram"), your rating and the time to Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) so we can improve the messages. This feedback is not linked to your name, email or Apple Account details, and it is never used for advertising or tracking.

Purchases: Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the app whether your subscription is active.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send you early access updates and product announcements
  • To deliver screen time reminders based on your configured timers
  • To improve the reminder messages, using anonymous thumbs up/down feedback

Data Storage and Security

Screen time data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or sell your app usage data, and Nudgy contains no third-party analytics or advertising. Email addresses collected through the website are stored securely and used only for communication about Nudgy.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple services only: Screen Time (on your device), iCloud/CloudKit for message updates and anonymous feedback, and the App Store for subscriptions. Our website may use an email delivery service for the early-access list. These services are bound by their own privacy policies.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your email from our mailing list at any time by contacting us. You can also unsubscribe from any email we send using the link provided in the email.

Children's Privacy

Nudgy is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@heynudgy.com.
